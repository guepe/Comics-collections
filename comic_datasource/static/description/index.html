Comic Datasource

Covers and metadata for your comics — automatically

Google Books, Open Library and the BnF (French legal deposit) fill in covers, synopses, page counts, publication dates, publishers and authors of your albums. Only empty fields are filled: what you typed is never overwritten.

One click

Complete an album instantly

On an album, Compléter les infos looks its edition up by ISBN (or by exact title) and fills the cover, synopsis, page count, dates, ISBN and authors that are still empty.

  • Notification of what was added
  • Authors matched by exact name
  • Google quota handled gracefully
Search

Find any album and import it

The search wizard queries the three sources at once by ISBN or title, shows covers, series, volume, publisher and ISBN, and imports the selected albums into your catalogue.

  • ISBN-10 and ISBN-13
  • Series and volume detected
  • Import of the selection
Series

Never miss a volume

For a series, Odoo compares what you have with what the sources know (using the number of published volumes when available) and lists the missing ones, ready to add.

  • Add to the catalogue or to your wishlist
  • Weekly detection on followed series
  • Batches to respect API limits
Settings

Simple configuration

Add an optional free Google Books API key to raise the daily quota, enable or disable Open Library and the BnF, test the connection — that's it.

  • Free Google Books key (optional)
  • Open Library and BnF without key
  • Configurable source order
