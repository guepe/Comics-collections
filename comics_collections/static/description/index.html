Comic Collection

Manage your comic book collection directly in Odoo 19.

Series, albums, authors, publishers, CSV/Excel import — everything the discerning collector needs.

Key features

📚 Collection

  • Manage your series and albums with cover images
  • Link authors to albums with their role: scriptwriter, artist, colorist, other
  • Dedicated models for publishers and genres
  • EAN-13 / ISBN-10 management with uniqueness validation

📖 Loans

  • Track who borrowed which album
  • Expected and effective return dates
  • Overdue loans highlighted automatically
  • Full chatter & activity tracking on albums and editions

📥 Import

  • Import from CSV, XLS or XLSX files
  • 4-step wizard: Upload → Mapping → Preview → Import
  • Downloadable file template
  • Detailed error report for failed rows

🛒 Purchase links

  • Club.be, Amazon.be, FNAC.be links generated automatically from the ISBN
  • One-click opening in a new tab
  • Weekly scheduled action fills in missing links

Kanban views with cover art

Browse your series and albums as a grid with large cover images. A colorful editorial placeholder is shown automatically when a cover is not yet available. Each series card shows the total number of albums (tomes).

Who is it for?

Any comic book collector who wants to manage their library from Odoo: series tracking, album inventory, authors, publishers, genres, and loans.

Compatible with Franco-Belgian comics, manga and American comics collections. Built for Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise.
