Comic Collection

Your comic books, manga and graphic novels - finally organised in Odoo

Series, albums, covers, authors and publishers; a private library for every user with wishlist, reading status and ratings; the missing volumes of the series you follow; loans, value and a printable inventory. Built for passionate collectors at home, ready to grow into a full comic shop.

Everything a collector needs

Series & albums

Cover galleries of your series and volumes, one-shots and hors-séries, editions, ISBN, publishers and authors with their roles.

Smart wishlist

Series you start are followed automatically: their missing volumes show up in your wishlist next to your own wishes.

Personal library

Owned or wished, read or in progress, star ratings, comments, purchase price and estimated value — per user.

Dashboard

Collection size and value, complete series, genres, reading progress, best-rated series and overdue loans at a glance.

Loans

Know who borrowed which album, with due dates, overdue highlighting and automatic reminders.

Import & clean-up

CSV / Excel import with column mapping and a duplicate detector to merge records safely.

Dashboard

Your collection at a glance

A dedicated dashboard sums up what you own and what you are looking for: number of albums and series, complete and ongoing series, wishlist, reading status and genres.

Add a purchase price and an estimated value to your albums to know what your collection is worth — then export it to CSV or print a PDF inventory, e.g. for your insurer.

  • Collection value and totals
  • Best-rated series and recent additions
  • Overdue loans
  • CSV export and PDF inventory
Series & albums

Beautiful cover galleries

My series and My albums open as cover galleries, grouped and sorted by series and volume. Each user only sees their own comics; the shared catalogue stays behind the scenes.

The series form gathers every author of its albums with their roles, and its volumes.

  • Unnumbered volumes and one-shots
  • Several editions and ISBN per album
  • Authors are regular Odoo contacts
  • Chatter and activities everywhere
Album form

All the details, one form

Create an album with its series, volume, title, publisher, authors and ISBN in a single form. ISBN-10 and EAN-13 are validated and converted; on a phone, scan the barcode with the camera.

Mark albums as read or in progress, add them to your wishlist in one click.

  • ISBN validation, conversion and uniqueness
  • Camera barcode scan on mobile
  • Purchase links (Club, Amazon, FNAC)
  • New-release notifications for followed series
Wishlist

Never miss a volume again

Start a series and it is followed automatically (unfollowing is remembered). My wishlist lists, series by series, the volumes you are missing plus the albums you wished for.

One click to say I have it, to ignore a volume you will not buy, or to stop following the series.

  • Missing volumes computed per user
  • Manual wishes
  • Upcoming releases filter
  • Shareable wishlist with Comic Shop
Library

Your personal library

Every album you own or wish for, with your star rating, reading status, comments, purchase price and estimated value — private to you, even when several people share the same Odoo.

  • Read / in progress / unread
  • 1 to 5 stars
  • Group by series, genre or source
  • Multi-user: shared catalogue, private collections
Authors

Who worked with whom

Authors are Odoo contacts with a comics tab: every series and album they worked on, their roles, and the artists they most often worked with — click through to their series.

  • Bibliography per author
  • Frequent collaborators
  • Publisher catalogues
  • Biography tab with the AI add-on
Import

Bring your existing list

Import a CSV, TSV, XLS or XLSX file: map the columns, choose whether existing albums are updated or skipped, download covers from a URL column and get a report with the real line numbers.

A duplicate detector (normalised titles and similarity score) proposes pairs to merge or ignore.

  • Column mapping step
  • Update or skip policy
  • Series matched on normalised names
  • Scored duplicate pairs, merge in either direction

Make it even more powerful

Data sources (free)

comic_datasource

Fill in covers, synopses, page counts, dates and authors from Google Books, Open Library and the BnF — one button per album, or a nightly job. Detects the missing volumes of followed series.

AI assistant (paid add-on)

comic_ai

Generate or translate synopses, author biographies and publisher histories with Claude or OpenAI, fill empty texts in the background, and discover new series from your 4★ favourites.

Comic Shop (paid add-on)

comic_shop

Sell your comics online and at the POS, and give every customer their own library on the portal, with series follow-up and release e-mails.

AI add-on

Synopses in seconds

With comic_ai, a manager generates a synopsis from what the catalogue knows (series, volume, authors, publisher), previews and edits it before applying — an existing text is never replaced without confirmation, and every change is logged.

  • Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI, your own API key
  • French, Dutch, English, German
  • Background filling of empty texts, marked for review
  • Discovery of new series
