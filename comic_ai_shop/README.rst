Glue module between *comic_ai* and *comic_shop*, installed automatically with both.

It brings the AI discovery by taste profile to the customers of the portal library
(``/my/library/discover``): suggestions of new series close to the ones they rated
4★ or more, verification on the data sources and addition to their wishlist. The
series missing from the catalogue are proposed through the moderated circuit of the
shop (records *à valider*). A daily limit per customer keeps the AI costs under
control.

It also shows the author biography and the publisher history (``comic_ai``) on the
public pages ``/shop/auteurs/<id>`` and ``/shop/editeurs/<id>``.

Configuration
=============

Configure the AI provider in *Settings → Bandes Dessinées → Intelligence
artificielle* (see *comic_ai*).

*Découverte IA sur le portail* sets the number of discovery searches allowed per
customer and per day (system parameter ``comic_ai.discovery_daily_limit``, default
``5``; ``0`` disables the discovery on the portal).

Usage
=====

On *Ma Bibliothèque BD* (``/my/library``), customers click *Découvrir de nouvelles
BD*:

1. The taste profile lists the series of their library; those with an album rated
   4★ or more are ticked. They adjust the selection and click *Obtenir des
   suggestions* (the remaining searches of the day are shown).
2. The suggested series (title, authors, genre, reason) exclude the series already in
   their collection or wishlist. *Vérifier sur les sources* checks a suggestion on the
   data sources.
3. They tick the series they like and click *Ajouter à ma wishlist*: the first volume
   is added to their wishlist. A series missing from the catalogue is created as a
   proposal to validate by the shop manager (*À valider (portail)*).
