On *Ma Bibliothèque BD* (``/my/library``), customers click *Découvrir de nouvelles
BD*:

1. The taste profile lists the series of their library; those with an album rated
   4★ or more are ticked. They adjust the selection and click *Obtenir des
   suggestions* (the remaining searches of the day are shown).
2. The suggested series (title, authors, genre, reason) exclude the series already in
   their collection or wishlist. *Vérifier sur les sources* checks a suggestion on the
   data sources.
3. They tick the series they like and click *Ajouter à ma wishlist*: the first volume
   is added to their wishlist. A series missing from the catalogue is created as a
   proposal to validate by the shop manager (*À valider (portail)*).
