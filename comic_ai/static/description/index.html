Comic AI

AI assistance for comic records, with Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI.

  • Settings: active provider, API keys and models, default generation language, connection test.
  • Synopsis assistant (album / edition): AI-generated synopsis and translation (fr / nl / en / de) with preview, explicit confirmation before replacing an existing synopsis and a chatter trace.

Configuration

Go to Settings → Bandes Dessinées → Intelligence artificielle:

  1. Choose the provider (Claude or OpenAI) and the default generation language.
  2. Enter the API key of the provider (stored as a system parameter, never in the code) and, if needed, the model (default claude-sonnet-5-5 / gpt-5-mini).
  3. Click Tester la connexion IA.

The Python libraries anthropic and openai are imported on demand: install the one of your provider on the server (pip install anthropic).

Usage

Synopsis generation and translation (comic managers)

  1. Open an album, a series (or an edition, in Configuration → Référentiel avancé) and go to the Synopsis tab. On an album, the assistant works on its main edition; on a series, on the series synopsis (written from its type, status, genre, publisher, authors and volumes; a translation replaces it).
  2. Click Générer le synopsis (or Traduire le synopsis when a synopsis exists).
  3. In the AI assistant, choose the action:
    • Générer le synopsis: a short synopsis (4 to 6 sentences, no major spoiler, no invented facts) written from the title, series, volume, authors with their roles, publisher and existing synopsis, in the chosen language (default: the language set in the settings).
    • Traduire le synopsis: translation of the current synopsis into French, Dutch, English or German. When the album has an edition in the target language, choose whether to replace the synopsis of the current edition or to write the translation on that edition.
  4. Click Générer (generation can take a few seconds; the screen is blocked meanwhile). The text is shown as a preview and can be edited.
  5. Appliquer writes it (converted to clean HTML) on the edition, Régénérer asks the AI for a new version, Annuler closes without any change. If the edition already has a synopsis, the old and new texts are shown side by side and Remplacer le synopsis existant must be ticked before applying.

Each application is logged in the edition chatter (action, provider, model). Provider errors (invalid key, timeout, quota…) are shown as clear messages.

The prompts are plain text files in comic_ai/prompts/ (synopsis.txt, translate.txt, serie_synopsis.txt) and can be adjusted without touching the code.

Discover new comics (AI taste profile)

Bandes Dessinées → Ma Collection → Découvrir de nouvelles BD opens a wizard:

  1. The taste profile lists the series of the albums you rated 4★ or more in your library. Remove or add series, then click Obtenir des suggestions. An empty profile gives a clear message (rate at least one album 4★).
  2. The AI (prompt comic_ai/prompts/discovery.txt) suggests 10 to 15 series you do not know yet: title, author(s), genre and a short reason, in the default generation language. The series already in your collection or wishlist (owned, wished or followed series) are excluded, in the prompt and again afterwards (normalized title comparison).
  3. Vérifier searches the series on the data sources (Google Books, Open Library, BnF) and shows what was found (publisher, ISBN of volume 1, authors): a guard against hallucinated titles.
  4. Tick the suggestions you like and click Ajouter à ma wishlist: the first volume of a series already in the catalogue is added to your wishlist; otherwise the series and its volume 1 are created (with the ISBN and publisher found by the verification, if any).

Provider errors (invalid key, timeout, quota, unreadable answer) are shown as clear messages. With comic_shop, the module comic_ai_shop offers the same discovery to the customers of the portal library.

Fill empty synopses in the background

In Settings → Bandes Dessinées → Intelligence artificielle, tick Remplir automatiquement les synopsis vides (disabled by default: each synopsis is a paid AI call). Every hour, the scheduled action BD — IA : synopsis vides (séries et albums) writes the empty synopses of active series and albums (main edition), by batches of Synopsis par passage (default 20), with the same prompt as the assistant and the mention (Généré automatiquement par IA). Existing synopses are never touched. The filter Synopsis généré par IA (à relire) lists them for review; editing a synopsis removes the flag. Lancer maintenant schedules an immediate run. A provider error (invalid key, quota) stops the current batch. With Inclure les biographies d’auteurs et les histoires des éditeurs (on by default), the same task then fills the empty biographies of credited authors and histories of publishers having editions (filter Biographie générée par IA (à relire) on contacts).

Author biography and publisher history

The author form has a Biographie tab and the publisher form a Histoire de la maison tab. Comic managers use Générer / Traduire there (same assistant as the synopsis: preview, confirmation before replacing, chatter trace marked “à relire”). The prompts give the AI the catalogue facts (roles, series, publishers, years, co-authors) and forbid uncertain facts. The author tab also lists the authors the person has often worked with.
