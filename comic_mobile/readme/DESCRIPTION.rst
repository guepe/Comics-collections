Installable mobile app (PWA) served by Odoo to manage your comic collection from your phone:

* **Scan an ISBN** with the camera (or type it): "You own it", "You're missing it" (with the
  volumes of the series you own), or "Not in the catalogue"; add it to your collection or
  wishlist in one tap.
* **My Collection**: cover grid with volume badges and reading status, search by title, series,
  author or ISBN, reading filters.
* **Series**: progress, follow / unfollow, owned and missing volumes, reading status.
* **My Wishlist**: missing volumes of the series you follow and your wishes, grouped by series,
  with upcoming releases.

It only shows the collection of the logged-in user (``comics_collections`` privacy rules).
