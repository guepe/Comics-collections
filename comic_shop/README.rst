Sell comic books online and at the counter, and give customers their own
library on the portal. Extends ``comics_collections`` and ``comic_datasource``.

**Product synchronisation**

A ``comic.edition`` can be linked to a ``product.template`` (*Créer produit*).
Name (``Série — Titre (T1)``), cover, description and barcode (ISBN-13) follow the
edition, and are resynchronised when the series, the album or an ISBN changes.
A barcode typed by hand is kept, and *Available in POS* is only set when the
product is created. Products can be bulk-created from a series for all editions
that have an ISBN. An ``uninstall_hook`` cleans up shop-specific data.

**Webshop pages**

``/shop/series``, ``/shop/auteurs`` and ``/shop/editeurs`` browse the comics on
sale (published and active products only), with filters on series type. An
author page only exists for partners credited on an album on sale.

**Customer library** (``/my/library``)

Each customer keeps their own library (``comic.customer.album``): *Ma collection*
and *Ma wishlist*, reading status, 0–5 rating and comments, with pagination and
sorting. Albums bought outside the shop are added by ISBN or title search
(local catalogue + online sources); cover downloads are restricted to the known
source hosts. Putting an owned album in the wishlist keeps it in the collection;
adding it to the collection removes it from the wishlist.

**Purchases**

Confirmed sale orders and paid POS orders add the albums to the buyer's library
(source *Acheté ici*), unless the customer switched this off on their library page.
Cancelled orders and POS refunds remove the entries they created.

**Moderation of portal records**

Series, albums and publishers created from the portal are flagged *À valider*
and a review activity is scheduled for a manager. Managers validate them, or
refuse them — as a duplicate of an existing album (the customer's library entry
and ISBN are moved onto it) or as incorrect (removed) — and the customer is
notified. Products cannot be created for albums awaiting validation.

Installation
============

This module depends on the following Odoo 19 modules (all standard):

- ``comics_collections``
- ``comic_datasource``
- ``sale``
- ``website``
- ``website_sale``
- ``point_of_sale``
- ``portal``

No extra Python packages are required beyond those needed by ``comic_datasource``.

Configuration
=============

1. Install ``comic_shop`` on top of ``comics_collections`` and ``comic_datasource``.
   A *Bandes Dessinées* POS category is created at installation.
2. In **Point of Sale → Configuration → Settings**, make sure the shop's POS
   includes the *Bandes Dessinées* category.
3. Set the sale price of comic products (products are created without a price).
4. Assign managers to the *Gestionnaire* comic group: they receive the review
   activities for records created from the portal.

Usage
=====

**Put an album on sale**

1. Open an edition and click *Créer produit* (or *Resynchroniser* to
   refresh an existing product). From a series, *À publier* creates the products
   of all its editions that have an ISBN.
2. Set the price and publish the product from the website backend.

**Point of Sale**

Scanning an ISBN selects the product. When a customer is set on the order, the
album is added to their library at payment.

**Customer portal**

Customers open *Ma Bibliothèque BD* (``/my/library``) from their portal, filter
collection / wishlist, update reading status and rating, and add albums with
*Ajouter une BD*. The *Ajouter automatiquement mes achats à ma bibliothèque*
switch disables the automatic addition of their purchases.

**Moderation**

*Comic Shop → À valider (portail)* lists the albums created by customers: use
*Valider*, or *Refuser* to open the refusal wizard (duplicate of an existing
album, or incorrect record, with an optional message to the customer).
*Comic Shop → Bibliothèques clients* lists all customer library entries.

**Data model**::

    comic.edition
      └── product_tmpl_id → product.template

    comic.customer.album        (unique per partner + edition)
      partner_id, edition_id, work_id (compute)
      source, etat_lecture, dans_collection, dans_wishlist
      note, commentaire, date_ajout
