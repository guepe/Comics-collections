Comic Shop

Sell comic books online and at the counter — and keep your customers coming back

Your albums become products in one click, with covers, descriptions and ISBN barcodes kept in sync. Customers browse series, authors and publishers on your webshop, keep their own comic library on the portal, follow their series and get an e-mail when the next volume is on sale.

Webshop

Album pages that sell

Every album on sale gets a rich product page: series and volume, authors with their roles, publisher, synopsis and cover — all coming from your catalogue, always up to date.

  • Series, author and publisher pages
  • Filters by series type
  • Only published products are shown
  • Author biographies with the AI add-on
Series

Follow a series, never miss a volume

Customers follow a series from the webshop or their library. When you put a new volume on sale, Odoo tells you how many customers are waiting for it.

  • Follow / unfollow from the portal
  • List of followed series
  • One e-mail per album and customer
Release e-mails

« Good news — it's available! »

Publish the albums of a series and the assistant shows the customers to notify — followers of the series and customers who have the album in their wishlist — with a preview of the e-mail.

  • Preview before sending
  • Never twice for the same album
  • Logged in the album chatter
Customer portal

A comic library for every customer

On My library, customers keep their collection and wishlist, reading status, star ratings and comments. Albums bought in your shop are added automatically; others are added by ISBN, title search or camera scan.

  • Collection and wishlist
  • Purchases added automatically (opt-out)
  • Search in your catalogue and online sources
  • Albums proposed by customers are moderated
Customer dashboard

Their collection, in numbers

Customers see how many albums and series they own, their complete series, reading progress, favourite genres and their best-rated series.

  • Personal statistics
  • Best-rated series
  • Recent additions
Add a comic

Scan the barcode with the phone

To add an album bought elsewhere, customers scan its ISBN with their phone camera, or type it. Odoo searches your catalogue first, then Google Books, Open Library and the BnF.

  • Camera scan (HTTPS)
  • Manual entry fallback
  • Covers from trusted sources only
Wishlist

Gift ideas, shared in one link

A customer creates a secret link to their wishlist and sends it to friends and family: a read-only page with the covers and a link to each album on sale in your shop. The link can be revoked at any time.

  • Read-only, no account needed
  • Links to your products
  • Revocable

AI add-on

Recommendations your customers will love

With comic_ai, customers get 10 to 15 series suggestions based on their favourites, can check each one against the data sources, and add them to their wishlist.

  • Taste profile from 4★ albums
  • Excludes what they already have
  • Daily limit per customer
