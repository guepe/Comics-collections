Comic Shop

Sell comic books online and at the counter, and give customers their own library on the portal. Extends comics_collections and comic_datasource.

It also adds the all comics layer: Catalogues → Tous les albums / Toutes les séries open the full shared catalogue to every comic user (without the shop, comics_collections is a private collection and keeps it for managers).

Product synchronisation

A comic.edition can be linked to a product.template (Créer produit). Name (Série — Titre (T1)), cover, description and barcode (ISBN-13) follow the edition, and are resynchronised when the series, the album or an ISBN changes. A barcode typed by hand is kept, and Available in POS is only set when the product is created. Products can be bulk-created from a series for all editions that have an ISBN. An uninstall_hook cleans up shop-specific data.

Webshop pages

/shop/series, /shop/auteurs and /shop/editeurs browse the comics on sale (published and active products only), with filters on series type. An author page only exists for partners credited on an album on sale.

Customer library (/my/library)

Each customer keeps their own library (comic.customer.album, the personal library of comics_collections): Ma collection and Ma wishlist, reading status, star rating and comments, a dashboard (/my/library/dashboard), with pagination and sorting. Albums bought outside the shop are added by ISBN or title search (local catalogue + online sources); cover downloads are restricted to the known source hosts. Putting an owned album in the wishlist keeps it in the collection; adding it to the collection removes it from the wishlist. A customer can share their wishlist through a secret, revocable link (/wishlist/<token>): a read-only page with covers and links to the albums on sale, nothing else.

Purchases

Confirmed sale orders and paid POS orders add the albums to the buyer’s library (source Acheté ici), unless the customer switched this off on their library page. Cancelled orders and POS refunds remove the entries they created.

Moderation of portal records

Series, albums and publishers created from the portal are flagged À valider and a review activity is scheduled for a manager. Managers validate them, or refuse them — as a duplicate of an existing album (the customer’s library entry and ISBN are moved onto it) or as incorrect (removed) — and the customer is notified. Products cannot be created for albums awaiting validation.

Installation

This module depends on the following Odoo 19 modules (all standard):

  • comics_collections
  • comic_datasource
  • sale
  • website
  • website_sale
  • point_of_sale
  • portal

No extra Python packages are required beyond those needed by comic_datasource.

Configuration

  1. Install comic_shop on top of comics_collections and comic_datasource. A Bandes Dessinées POS category is created at installation.
  2. In Point of Sale → Configuration → Settings, make sure the shop’s POS includes the Bandes Dessinées category.
  3. Set the sale price of comic products (products are created without a price).
  4. Assign managers to the Gestionnaire comic group: they receive the review activities for records created from the portal.

Usage

Put an album on sale

  1. Open an edition and click Créer produit (or Resynchroniser to refresh an existing product). From a series, À publier creates the products of all its editions that have an ISBN.
  2. Set the price and publish the product from the website backend.
  3. Tell the customers: when customers follow the series or have the album in their wishlist, the edition shows N client(s) attendent cet album → Prévenir les clients. The wizard shows how many customers will be notified, the recipients and a preview of the email, then sends it (once per album and customer, trace in the edition chatter). Publier les albums on a series opens the same wizard; Prévenir les clients (mise en vente) is also in the action menu of series and editions.

Point of Sale

Scanning an ISBN selects the product. When a customer is set on the order, the album is added to their library at payment.

Customer portal

Customers open Ma Bibliothèque BD (/my/library) from their portal, filter collection / wishlist, update reading status and star rating, open their Tableau de bord BD (/my/library/dashboard), share their wishlist (Partager ma wishlist → link to copy, revocable), and add albums with Ajouter une BD (the Scanner button reads the ISBN barcode with the phone camera; HTTPS is required, manual entry stays available). Mes séries (/my/library/series) lists the followed series and those of the library, with Suivre la série / Série suivie buttons, also shown on the shop series page and on an album of the library. The Ajouter automatiquement mes achats à ma bibliothèque switch disables the automatic addition of their purchases.

Moderation

Comic Shop → À valider (portail) lists the albums created by customers: use Valider, or Refuser to open the refusal wizard (duplicate of an existing album, or incorrect record, with an optional message to the customer). Comic Shop → Bibliothèques clients lists all customer library entries.

Data model:

comic.edition
  └── product_tmpl_id → product.template

comic.customer.album        (defined in comics_collections, extended here)
  + source « achete_ici », sale_order_line_id, pos_order_line_id
  + auto_created, has_product
